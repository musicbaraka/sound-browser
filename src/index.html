Sound Browser

Find, hear, shape and organize every sound you own. Drop in a folder and the app builds a searchable library of everything inside it, then lets you edit any sound in seconds and drag the result wherever you need it.

· Windows · Updates install automatically

Your library

  • Drop in any folder. Every subfolder is scanned.
  • WAV, AIFF, MP3, FLAC, OGG, M4A and more.
  • Folders are remembered. New sounds appear on their own.
  • Length, sample rate, bit depth and channels for every file.

Find anything

  • Search names, folders, tags, keys and tempo.
  • Spelling help: "snair" finds snare, "forrest" finds forest.
  • Filter by type, key, BPM range, tag or favorites.
  • Browse with the arrow keys and hear each sound instantly.

Key and tempo (optional)

  • Off by default. Right-click a folder and choose Detect key and tempo here.
  • Musical key and BPM are detected by listening, in the background.
  • Keys and tempos written in file names or tags take priority.
  • Results are kept, even when it is turned off.

Quick edit

  • Select any part on a zoomable waveform.
  • Change speed and pitch separately, or together like tape.
  • Volume, fades, reverse and normalize.
  • Drag the highlighted part straight out of the waveform into any folder or your DAW. Originals are never changed.
  • Quick edit: drop any sound file from anywhere onto the waveform. It opens for editing without being added to your library.

Clean

  • Noise reduction for hiss, hum and room tone.
  • Learn the noise from a selection, or let it estimate the noise.
  • Hear the result live while you adjust it.

Analyze

  • Live spectrum analyzer with peak hold.
  • Hover to read frequency, musical note and level.
  • Spectrogram view of any sound, alone or together with the waveform.
  • Average spectrum to compare before and after an edit.

Organize

  • Add your own tags to any sound, or to every sound shown at once.
  • Generate new sound effects from a description with your ElevenLabs account. They are saved and tagged automatically.
  • Favorites with one key.
  • Resize every column like a spreadsheet, and the sidebar, waveform and spectrum panels.
  • Your last folder, search, filters, layout and settings come back every time.
